   bounded native readiness before diagnosing a performance cause; do not call
   wallpaper a panel render or change VM settings to manufacture evidence.

key behavior and frame geometry stay unchanged, descriptions fit EN/RU, theme
and original assets are retained, and only local draft/validation operations are
